About

Hey, I'm Aisha.

I'm a product manager, writer, and media builder obsessed with making information easier, and more enjoyable, to take in. I live at the intersection of news, internet culture, business, and storytelling.

Right now I'm building projects that help people stay informed without feeling overwhelmed, starting with a media brand that treats the news like a mixtape instead of a chore.

"Make it easier to stay informed, not heavier."
Aisha Fesola Onola, founder of The OffScript
The Brand

The OffScript

Founded 2026 · soundtrack journalism

News, reimagined as soundtrack journalism. The OffScript pairs the stories that matter with the music that fits them, so staying informed feels less like a chore and more like a mixtape you didn't know you needed. I write it, produce it, and run the socials and newsletter design myself.
